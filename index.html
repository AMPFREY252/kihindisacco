Welcome to KIHINDI SACCO

Empowering Mbarara's Boda Boda Riders Community

KIHINDI SACCO is a dedicated financial cooperative serving Mbarara City's boda boda riders. We provide:

  • Savings and investment opportunities
  • Accessible loans for business growth
  • Financial security and protection
  • Community-driven decision making
